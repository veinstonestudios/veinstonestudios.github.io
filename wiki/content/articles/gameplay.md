**Gameplay** in *[[The Facility]]* places the player in the role of **[[Henry Edaven]]**, the Warden of [[Facility 61]]. The Warden's responsibility is to **interrogate inmates, evaluate their humanity, and select individuals for daily [[Assignments|assignments]]**.

:::dev
*The Facility* is still in development. Mechanics described here are based on official material and may change before release.
:::

## Overview
Hidden among the inmates of Facility 61 are [[The Corrupted]], anomalies capable of imitating humans. The player must work out who is human and who is not — and decide who is sent out of the facility.

## The daily cycle
According to the official press kit, each in-game day consists of four phases:

| Phase | Description |
|---|---|
| **Orders** | The State faxes the Warden's quota at dawn — how many inmates are required, and for what work. See [[Assignments]]. |
| **Interrogation** | The Warden questions inmates face to face and inspects their documents for discrepancies. See [[Tests & Detection Methods]]. |
| **Selection** | The Warden decides who ships out. Sending a human into a death trap wastes a resource; sending an anomaly is worse. |
| **Night** | The Warden's decisions come back. Scripted night events follow the player's choices, and the player must survive until morning. See [[Night Events]]. |

## Tools of the Warden
Official screenshots and press material show several tools available to the Warden:
- **Cell inspection** — observing an inmate through the hatch of their cell.
- **The journal** — used to cross-reference testimony.
- **The tape recorder** — used to review recorded interrogations.
- **Inmate selection** — choosing which inmates to deploy.

See [[Tests & Detection Methods]] for how these are used to identify the Corrupted.

## Consequences
If the Warden fails to identify an anomaly and sends it on an assignment, the State holds the Warden responsible for the consequences. The full range of consequences, and how they affect the story and [[Endings]], has not been documented. {{tba}}
