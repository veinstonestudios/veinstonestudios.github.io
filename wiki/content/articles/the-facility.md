***The Facility*** is a psychological horror game developed by Veinstone Studios. It is set in the year 2055, in a world where advanced technology and artificial intelligence have been forbidden following [[The Technology Purge]].

The player takes the role of **[[Henry Edaven]]**, the Warden of **[[Facility 61]]** — a prison and rehabilitation center where convicted individuals suspected of being [[The Corrupted|Corrupted]] are held. The Warden's responsibility is to interrogate inmates, evaluate their humanity, and select individuals for daily [[Assignments|assignments]].

:::note Not to be confused with
*The Facility* is the title of the game. **[[Facility 61]]** is the name of the main location within the game.
:::

## Overview
*The Facility* combines psychological horror with an analog horror aesthetic. Its world is retro-futuristic: although the game takes place in 2055, society has returned to analog methods of communication and information processing during [[The Analog Era]].

Official press material describes the game as a psychological horror strategy game with a retro PSX-inspired aesthetic, in which the player manages a rehabilitation center for subjects required by the State for dangerous reconstruction missions.

:::quote
Trust no one. Anomalies are hiding among your inmates.
— Official tagline
:::

## Premise
Hidden among the inmates of Facility 61 are [[The Corrupted]] — anomalies capable of imitating humans. It is not always easy to distinguish a real human from a Corrupted individual. The player must identify these threats before they are sent out into the world on assignments.

The protagonist's personal history is closely tied to the Corruption. Details are covered in the [[Henry Edaven]] and [[Facility 60]] articles, which contain spoilers.

## Content warnings
According to the official press kit, *The Facility* includes:
- Frequent violence or gore — blood and dead bodies in a retro PS1 art style.
- General mature content — disturbing sounds, flashing lights, and creature designs intended to frighten the player.
- A threatening environment where the player is in danger from hostile entities.
