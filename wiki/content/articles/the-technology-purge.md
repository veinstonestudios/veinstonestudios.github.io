**The Technology Purge** is a historical event in the world of *[[The Facility]]*. Following the Purge, **advanced technology and artificial intelligence became forbidden**, and society returned to more primitive, analog methods of communication and information processing — the beginning of [[The Analog Era]].

## Overview
The Purge is the defining event of the game's world. [[The Corrupted]] originate from the very advanced artificial intelligence and technology that became forbidden following the Purge.
