**Assignments** are a core part of *[[The Facility]]*. Each day, the Warden of [[Facility 61]] selects inmates to be sent out of the facility on assignments.

:::dev
*The Facility* is still in development. Details on this page are based on official material and may change before release.
:::

## Overview
According to the official press kit, the State requires subjects from Facility 61 for dangerous reconstruction missions. The Warden must choose which inmates to send — while making sure that no [[The Corrupted|Corrupted]] individual is among them.

## Orders
At dawn, the State faxes the Warden a quota stating **how many** inmates are required and **for what work**. The quota should be read carefully before any selection is made.

## Selection
After the [[Tests & Detection Methods|interrogations]], the Warden decides who ships out.

:::warning Consequences
- Sending a human into a death trap wastes a resource.
- Sending an anomaly is worse: if the Warden fails to identify an anomaly and sends it on an assignment, the State holds the Warden responsible for the consequences.
:::
