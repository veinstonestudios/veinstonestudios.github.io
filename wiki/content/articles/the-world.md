**The World** of *[[The Facility]]* is set in the year **2055**. Following [[The Technology Purge]], advanced technology and artificial intelligence have been banned, and society has returned to more primitive, analog methods of communication and information processing — a period known as **[[The Analog Era]]**.

## Overview
Official press material describes the setting as "a world on the brink of collapse". The world is retro-futuristic: despite the year, its technology is analog, institutional and bureaucratic.

The defining threat of this world is **[[The Corrupted]]** — anomalies capable of imitating humans, originating from the very advanced artificial intelligence and technology that is now forbidden.

## The State
According to the official press kit, the governing authority in the game is referred to as **the State**. The State requires subjects from [[Facility 61]] for dangerous reconstruction missions, and at the start of each day sends the Warden a quota of inmates required for work. See [[Assignments]].

If the Warden fails to identify a Corrupted individual and sends them on an assignment, the State holds the Warden responsible for the consequences.

Further details about the State's structure and history have not been documented. {{tba}}

## Facilities
Individuals suspected of being Corrupted are held in numbered institutions. Two are currently known:
- **[[Facility 61]]** — the prison and rehabilitation center where the game takes place.
- **[[Facility 60]]** — a previous facility, shut down after being overrun by anomalies.

Whether other numbered facilities exist has not been confirmed. {{unknown}}
